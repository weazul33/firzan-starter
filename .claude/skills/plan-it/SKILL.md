---
name: plan-it
description: Propose a short plan before anything gets built: version 1 first, then each new change.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Don't create or change any files during this command, except NOTES.md and HABITS.md (the scorecard) in step 4, or CLAUDE.md when an idea is parked under "Not in version 1".

1. If they named a change (in their message or after the command), plan that change, unless it needs more than a plain page (a shop, a contact form, a sign-up, or anything marked "(needs more than a plain page)"). Then don't plan it: follow the plain-page rule in .claude/CLAUDE.md and stop. If version 1 isn't finished and the change they named isn't in "Done looks like", offer to park it under "Not in version 1" instead (the scope trap), and plan what's left of version 1. Otherwise read "Done looks like" and "Current status" in CLAUDE.md. If version 1 isn't finished, plan what's left of it. If it is finished, ask one question: "What would you like to change today?" If the page has no colours or font yet, mention that this is one option. A change from "Not in version 1" is fine to plan once version 1 is done, unless it's marked "(needs more than a plain page)".
2. Propose a plan of no more than five numbered steps (one or two is fine for a small change). For each step, say in one line what the learner will be able to see or click when it's done.
3. Ask: "Is there any step you'd cut or move to later?" At coaching level full, add one line on why: smaller plans are easier to check and easier to undo.
4. When they're happy, add the agreed plan under "Plan" in NOTES.md, below a short heading with the session number and the change. Keep earlier plans. Add any new words to "Words I've learned" in the same change. Ask permission first like any file change. Then count habit 2 in HABITS.md: under "On my own" if they typed /plan-it without you suggesting it in your reply just before, otherwise under "Reminded."
5. Say: "When you're ready, tell me 'Do step 1.'"
