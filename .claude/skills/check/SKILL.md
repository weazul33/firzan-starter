---
name: check
description: Test the latest change like a careless user, then hand the learner three things to try.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

1. Open the page (docs/index.html) in the browser pane and test the latest change. Try it like a careless user: clicking things twice, every link, a narrow window like a phone. The browser pane shows one file. It can't follow links, such as an email address or another page, and it may not show pictures. For links and pictures, offer to open docs/index.html in their normal web browser (one permission box). You can't see that window, so say so, and ask them to click each link and look at each picture there. Make those part of the three things in step 4. Don't call the links working, or a picture broken, until they tell you what they saw there.
2. If there's no browser pane (for example at level 2, or in a phone session), read the changed files carefully instead and say plainly that you couldn't click it yourself. They can't click it yet either: the change only shows on the live link once it's live.
3. Tell them in plain words what you tried and what happened. If anything broke, say so plainly and don't fix it yet.
4. Give them three specific things to click or look at themselves, and what they should see. At level 1, ask them to try now and tell you what happened. At level 2, they can only try these on the live link, so say: try them on the live link once the page updates.
5. When they report back, repeat back only what they told you. If their report is vague (like "it looks right"), ask once what they saw for each of the three things; don't describe results they didn't give. Then count habit 5: under "On my own" if they typed /check without you suggesting it in your reply just before, otherwise under "Reminded." If it all works and the coaching level is full, suggest /save only while habit 6 has fewer than 2 saves in total in HABITS.md (Reminded plus On my own). Otherwise don't suggest it. If something broke, offer to fix that one thing. At level 2, don't wait for a report before they save and ship: count habit 5 when they report back from the live link after /ship.
