Don't build anything yet. Don't touch docs/ until the brief is approved.

1. If the brief is already filled in, say what it says and suggest /pickup instead. Stop.
2. Ask: "Is this your first project with me?" If not, set Coaching level to light when you save the brief.
3. Offer the menu, one line each, and say which you'd pick:
   - A personal page about you: what you do, what you're into, a way to reach you that you're happy to share publicly. (My pick for a first build.)
   - A page for a hobby or club.
   - A page for an event or trip.
   All three are one page that opens in a web browser and can go online later.
4. Ask these one at a time to decide what goes on the page:
   - "Who would you want to send this page to?"
   - "What do people usually ask you about?"
   - "What are three things you'd want on it?"
5. Check the size: version 1 should be describable in two sentences and buildable in about an hour.
6. Fill in the brief one question at a time, in their own words. Don't add ideas they didn't give you; if you want to suggest one, ask first. Remind them it may be public later: no home address, phone number or anything private.
7. Show the finished brief and ask them to approve it before you save it. Name habit 1 (giving context) in one line, and count it under "Reminded" in HABITS.md when you save the brief, because you asked the questions.
8. Right after saving, give a short tour of the project, one line each:
   - A file ending in .md is a plain text file you can read, like a note.
   - CLAUDE.md, at the top of the folder, is your brief. You can ask me to change it any time.
   - NOTES.md holds the plan, the log and the words you've learned. HABITS.md is your scorecard.
   - The docs folder is your website. versions/ and set-aside/ will hold your saves and anything we set aside.
   - There's also a hidden .claude folder with my coaching rules, including a second CLAUDE.md. That one runs the project, so I can't change it, and you don't need to.
9. Say: "Next, type /plan-it. Stuck at any point? Type /stuck. Don't know a word? Type /explain. (/help opens Anthropic's help website, not me.)"
